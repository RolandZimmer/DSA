How this run works

Expected O(1) operations need a well-distributed hash and controlled load. Worst case is O(n). Use Resize & rehash to move all live keys to a new table. Each rebuild costs O(n) with good distribution and enough capacity; geometric growth gives expected amortized O(1) insertion.
